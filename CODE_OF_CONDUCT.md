# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a constructive place to build and learn. Participate with empathy,
technical honesty, patience, and respect for different backgrounds and experience levels.

Helpful behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- giving actionable, specific review feedback;
- respecting privacy, attribution, licenses, and creative ownership;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- making room for contributors who communicate in different languages or styles.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate exposure of secrets or personal data, license evasion, impersonation, and sustained
disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, pull requests, reviews, project chat, events, and
public representation of the project. It also applies when someone uses an official role or project
identity elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary technical disagreement, use the relevant issue or pull request. For harassment, privacy
concerns, or another sensitive conduct report, contact `ben@tokimi.space` rather than posting
personal details publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, close discussions, or ban an account
when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, but
confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is required for safety or law. Retaliation
against a good-faith reporter is not accepted.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, repair, and community safety.
This project may decline participation even where conduct does not violate a law or platform rule.
